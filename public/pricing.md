# Pricing & Plans — CityRock POS

All plans include full POS offline cashier terminal, USB barcode scanner support, and real-time stock sync.

## Starter Plan
- Price: PKR 5,000 / month (or PKR 50,000 / year with 17% annual discount)
- Capacity: 1 Store Location, up to 3 Cashier staff accounts
- Products: Unlimited catalog items and variants
- Features:
  - High-speed POS billing terminal
  - Thermal receipt printing (58mm & 80mm ESC/POS)
  - Barcode scanner integration
  - Basic inventory tracking and sales reports
  - Excel and PDF export

## Pro Plan (Most Popular)
- Price: PKR 12,000 / month (or PKR 120,000 / year with 17% annual discount)
- Capacity: Up to 3 Store Locations, unlimited staff accounts
- Products: Unlimited catalog items and variants
- Features:
  - Everything in Starter
  - Multi-branch inventory transfer and central stock visibility
  - Real-time hourly sales analytics and top product ranks
  - Customer loyalty point system
  - Priority support and dedicated onboarding assistance

## Enterprise / Multi-Chain
- Price: Custom Quote — contact sales@cityrock.pk
- Capacity: 4+ Store Locations, dedicated database clusters
- Features: Custom ERP integrations, white-labeled receipts, custom SLA.
